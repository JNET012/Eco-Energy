About us

Eco Energy Plumbing & Renewables is a plumbing, heating and renewables company based in Bleadon, North Somerset, working with homeowners and landlords across the South West.

We’re Gas Safe registered and OFTEC certified, and our work is led by an NVQ Level 3 qualified engineer with more than 16 years in the trade.

We cover everything from boiler services and landlord certificates to full bathroom refits and heat pump installs. Whatever the size of the job, you’ll get the same care, a tidy finish and honest advice on what will and won’t work in your home.
